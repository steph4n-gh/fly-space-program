**Delivered software at the research cutoff.** [Fly Space Program](https://fly.steph4n.dev/)
was live as version 12 with owner-only access. The 27-mission simulator,
mission/cockpit/lab interface, animated fly, sampled-eye display, neural

The model makes two complete recurrent passes through **166,700 retained
neurons and 25,582,938 directed connections** per decision. The anatomical
source represents 124,177,617 synaptic contacts. All 2,129 descending/motor
output neurons contribute to a learned ten-command readout. Its 1,558 inputs
comprise two 32 × 24 modeled eyes, eighteen body channels and four odor
channels. Flight indicators present measurements. The learned pilot's
commands do not come from a language model or the separate instrument pilot.
See [controller and provenance](controller-and-provenance.md).

**Broader ground learning.** The six-generation all-ground lesson completed
3,456 training flights. Its later 384-flight comparison covered all 24 ground
missions and both sensory controls. The new controller landed **64/96**
against the release's **62/96**, but fell to **25/40 versus 28/40** on the
released ten missions. It failed the retention requirement and was rejected.
The small overall gain did not justify replacing previously learned skills.
The [full comparison](ground-all-selection.md) retains every paired outcome.

The separate [ground-contact experiment](ground-contact-comparison.md)
completed **4,608 training flights and 96 reserved comparisons**. Ranking
failed contacts by proximity to safe landing conditions produced **40/48
landings versus 36/48** for the control on fresh matched development starts.
It rescued six control failures and lost two control successes; 34 pairs
landed in both arms and six failed in both. Nominal and varied results were
20/24 each for the treatment, against 18/24 each for the control.

The gain is uneven: Night shift regressed from 2/2 to 0/2, while Absolutely
nominal, Wind shear and Tight storm remained 0/2. The treatment's eight
failures comprise four lateral impacts and four missed ships. The ranking
change retained the same seventeen learned directions, reward, sensory
representation and landing criteria. Both arms began from the earlier G6
development checkpoint, not the released G5 pilot, so this is **not a direct
comparison with the release**. One optimizer seed and two starts per mission
do not establish reliability or retention. No new controller was installed.

The original training and evaluation processes all completed successfully.
The prepared record audits passed 591,502 training checks and 138,324
comparison checks. All 4,704 endpoints, failures, rankings, optimizer states
and 48 pairs are retained in the [complete export](ground-contact-comparison/summary.json).
These checks validate stored records and arithmetic; they do not independently
replay flight physics or supply unrecorded terminal yaw measurements.

**Orbital learning.** Two recent experiments are complete: the
[588-flight progress-ranking comparison](orbital-progress-comparison.md) and
the [156-flight proposal-size comparison](orbital-local-comparison.md).
Neither produced a stable orbit, completed orbit or orbital landing. Both
retained their starting controller. In the proposal-size comparison, smaller
perturbations preserved some useful training behavior more often, but the
selected controllers were identical. All six reserved pairs produced exactly
matching recorded endpoints and trajectories and failed by leaving the
recovery corridor. All failures remain in the evidence.

Positive periapsis progress, reaching space or a larger training reward is
not successful orbital insertion. The current search has not solved the
three orbital missions. A possible three-generation continuation was
identified, but **no continuation plan, launcher or simulation was created**.
Only its completed training diagnosis was retained when the user requested
the wrap-up.

**Biology and chemical experiments.** The computational work has produced
useful constraints, but **no confirmed chemical steering intervention**:

- The [148-trial additional-odor panel](additional-odor-tests.md), including
  terpenes and an independent limonene confirmation, produced no compound
  that passed the predefined steering-follow-up requirement.
- The [68-trial calibration follow-up](odor-calibration-followup.md) reduced
  excessive projection-neuron activity at lower model connection weights,
  while eliminating recorded motor output there. A connection weight is
  not a chemical dose.
- Visual-pathway diagnostics recorded voltage responses and background-
  dependent activity changes, but did not establish functional visual-motion
  transmission under the inherited spiking-model rules. L1/L2 normally use
  graded signaling; lack of spikes alone is not evidence of a biological
  defect.
- The first [empirical visual-response benchmark](visual-response-benchmark.md)
  failed its independent-stimulus transfer test despite a high correlation.
  Absolute prediction error remained worse than the zero-response reference.
- All 32 [recurrent-model development fits](visual-recurrent-development.md)
  completed. Mean fitting MSE improved by 2.80% for L1 and 4.02% for L2.
  Twelve of sixteen chemical-response direction comparisons agreed; four
  bright-flash comparisons disagreed. These were development comparisons,
  not independent biological validation. The original transfer failure
  remains unresolved.

No fitted visual parameters were installed in the connectome. Accurate
stimulus calibration, appropriate transmission dynamics and independent
response data remain necessary before interpreting chemical modulation as
useful control. See the [biology research record](biology-experiments.md).

**What the biology claims mean.** The flight controller is a signed rate-style
model with dimensionless activity, not a measured reconstruction of firing
rates or a living fly. Anatomical connections remain fixed; software learns
the external decoder. The Brian2 spiking experiments are separate from the
controller that produced the landing result. Biological plasticity,
chemical efficacy and transfer to living animals remain unproven.

**Physical Fly Cube.** The proposed five trays of twenty-five cells are
concept art and an apparatus design, not built hardware. Automatic loading,
individual calibration, measurement quality and an advantage from combining
125 flies have not been demonstrated. The next physical research milestone
would be one instrumented cell and a repeatable visual-response/closed-loop
assay. [Concept gallery](harness-concept.md) and
[physical-interface boundary](physical-fly-interface.md).

**Repository and preservation.** The requested
No Theo License v1.0 is installed; this is source-available rather than an
open-source license. Third-party code and anatomical data retain their own
licenses and attribution. The documentation includes the Omarchy influence
and the stated commitments to honor, strength, courage, duty, justice,
beauty and truth.

Tracked application assets, reports and scripts are on GitHub. Large raw
datasets, frozen runtimes and full experiment archives remain local and are
excluded from Git. A clone can run the packaged simulator but is not a
complete copy of every research input. Those local archives should be
preserved for later work; see [reproduction and archive availability](reproducibility.md).

**Remaining goal.** A perceiving fly model that passes the entire mission
suite has **not** been achieved. Ground skill retention, the difficult
remaining landings, stable insertion, a complete orbit and safe orbital
return remain open. Biological calibration and physical transfer are
separate unfinished research requirements. Further experiments require a
new instruction after this requested stopping point.
